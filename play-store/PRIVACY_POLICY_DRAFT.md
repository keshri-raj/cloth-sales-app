# Avani Suto Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 25, 2026

Avani Suto is a shop-management app for authorized adult shop owners and staff. This policy explains what information the app processes and how it is used.

## Information the app processes

- **Staff account information:** the Google account email used to sign in and verify that the person is authorized to use the shop app.
- **Customer information:** customer name and phone number, plus email address and address when entered.
- **Shop and transaction information:** product names, variants, SKUs, categories, stock, prices, bills, quantities, discounts, payment amounts and methods, balances due, and order requests.
- **Product images:** images selected for catalog products. The shop backend stores these with the shop's product records.
- **Local drafts and cache:** the app may keep unsent form drafts and cached shop data on the device so staff can resume work and use the app more reliably.

The app does not ask for contact-list access as a general permission. When a staff member chooses a contact, Android's contact picker provides the selected information to the app.

## How information is used

Information is used to authenticate authorized staff and operate the shop's catalog, billing, inventory, customer, payment, and order workflows. The shop's backend uses Google Apps Script, Google Sheets, and Google Drive to process and store shop records and invoice PDFs.

The app does not include advertising SDKs in its current Android build. Staff may choose to share an invoice PDF through WhatsApp or another compatible app; that transfer is initiated by the staff member and is then handled by the selected app under its own policies.

## Storage, access, and security

Shop records are stored in the shop owner's Google Sheets and Drive account and are accessible to staff accounts authorized by the shop administrator. The app communicates with the backend over HTTPS. Some drafts and cached records may also be stored on the device.

No system can guarantee absolute security. The shop administrator is responsible for limiting access to the Google accounts, spreadsheet, Drive folder, and devices used for shop operations.

## Retention and deletion

Shop records stay in the shop's Google Sheets and Drive until the administrator removes them. Some administrator-reviewed removal or cancellation workflows retain an audit record rather than erase the original record. Local drafts can be cleared in the app or removed by clearing its device data/uninstalling it.

For access, correction, or deletion requests concerning shop records, contact the shop administrator at **keshrirajcp@gmail.com**. The administrator may need to retain transaction records for business or legal requirements.

## Children

Avani Suto is intended for adult shop owners and staff. It is not directed to children.
